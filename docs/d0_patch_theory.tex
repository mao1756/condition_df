\section{The D0-v0 fixed-grid model}

\paragraph{Scope.}
D0-v0 is a practical positive-time model on a fixed $28\times28$ simplex.
It does not claim a nonsingular continuum or time-zero limit. Its first
milestone is deliberately only the reconstruction of one MNIST image.

\paragraph{Reference and reversal.}
Let $\nu$ be the symmetric Dirichlet reference with concentration
$\alpha_{\mathrm{eff}}=1$ per cell, and let $p_\tau$ be the positive-time
forward marginal. On the finite grid, write
\[
  v_\tau(s)=\frac{dp_\tau}{d\nu}(s),
  \qquad f_\tau(s)=\log v_\tau(s).
\]
For the reversible reference process, the conditioning part of the reverse
flux on an oriented edge $i\to j$ is
\[
  J^{\mathrm{cond}}_{ij}
  =2N\,a(\tau)\,\theta_{ij}(s)
    \left(\partial_{s_j}f_\tau-\partial_{s_i}f_\tau\right),
\]
where $N=28^2$, $\theta$ is the established harmonic mobility, and $a$ is the
reference time-change rate. The existing limiter-aware reverse kernel adds
this flux to the known reversible drift and shared reference noise.

\paragraph{Density-ratio learning.}
At a matched $(\tau,y)$, positives are forward-noised states from the single
image and negatives are independent samples from
$\operatorname{Dirichlet}(\alpha_{\mathrm{eff}}\mathbf 1)$. A balanced
logistic classifier has population logit $f_\tau$ up to the balanced class
constant. The implementation uses the existing boundary-smooth scalar
potential with the normalized spatial-mean head. Sampling differentiates the
raw logit with respect to the simplex state and converts it to physical flux.

\paragraph{Time-zero convention.}
MNIST data are singular and sparse at time zero. Training excludes time zero,
and reverse sampling stops before the final zero-time substep. D0-v0 therefore
tests only the fixed-grid positive-time construction.

\paragraph{Decision rule.}
The first production run is evaluated once against coarse gates for finite
simplex-preserving sampling, held-out density-ratio BCE, correlation
improvement, and relative $L^1$ reduction. Failure publishes the samples and
summary and stops. It does not authorize seed searches, estimator changes, or
threshold optimization.

\paragraph{Deferred work.}
Exact Jacobi targets, certification and replay workflows, continuum
refinement, full-MNIST training, class priors, guidance, additional seeds, and
micro-accuracy metric optimization are outside D0-v0. The pre-v0 theory is
preserved in the repository's documentation archive.

\section{Exact Jacobi/Rao--Blackwell coarse-residual gate}

\paragraph{Restricted fixed-grid claim.}
The additive Jacobi investigation keeps the grid at $28\times28$,
$K=512$, and the certified Rao--Blackwell label $\bar Z$. Its terminal
coarse witness uses two independent 64-path panels and detects
\[
  S=\operatorname{mean}_{c}(a_cb_c)
   =0.0006484248701021389>0,
\]
where $c$ indexes reverse-time quartile, split phase, and oriented edge.
This is evidence for a nonzero coarse conditional mean for one frozen image,
not a continuum or reconstruction result.

\paragraph{Frozen shrinkage baseline.}
With $a$ and $b$ the two panel means, define
\[
  N=\frac12\operatorname{mean}_{c}(a_c-b_c)^2,\qquad
  \lambda=\frac{S}{S+N/2},\qquad
  B_c=\lambda\frac{a_c+b_c}{2}.
\]
Numerically, $N=0.00315904482822984$,
$\lambda=0.2910413880506186$, and
$\operatorname{mean}(B^2)=0.00018871847424106853$. The rule is frozen
before fresh neural evidence is generated and retains every signed cell
value.

\paragraph{Unchanged denoising objective.}
The new model has the form
\[
  g_\theta(W)=B(C(W))+r_\theta(W)
\]
and minimizes
\[
  \frac{\operatorname{mean}
    \bigl(B(C(W))+r_\theta(W)-\bar Z\bigr)^2}{c^2},
  \qquad
  c^2=\operatorname{mean}_{\rm train}\bar Z^2.
\]
Thus the baseline is an initialization and control variate, not a replacement
target. The exact label is not clipped, weighted, residualized on disk, or
otherwise modified. Both residual output paths start at exactly zero, so
update zero equals the frozen baseline.

\paragraph{Fresh paired-risk decision.}
After validation-only checkpoint selection, one untouched 64-path panel
reports
\[
 \Delta_B=R(0)-R(B),\qquad
 \Delta_R=R(B)-R(B+r_\theta).
\]
A one-sided 99\% whole-path studentized max-$T$ family must place strictly
positive simultaneous lower bounds on both contrasts. Passing establishes
only held-out predictive learnability for this image and split chain. It
authorizes planning a separate reverse-phase sampler control, not sampling,
reconstruction, a known prior, unsplit-generator convergence, or spatial
Dirichlet--Ferguson convergence.

\section{Boundary-tangent Jacobi/RB controller}

\paragraph{Affine-flow failure.}
The first reverse-controller implementation froze the conormal conditional
mean $m$ during each learned substep and applied
\[
  y^+=y+2m\,\delta u.
\]
Although the exact Jacobi reference half-transition remained certified, it
could put $y$ arbitrarily close to a facet. A nonzero frozen affine increment
then left $[0,1]$ at $M=2,4,8$ (and in an advisory $M=16$ replay). This is a
finite-step coordinate failure, not evidence against the exact Jacobi law or
the Rao--Blackwell target.

\paragraph{Tangent parameterization and unchanged target.}
For earlier and later head fractions $X,Y$, let $H=L-MY$ be the ancestral
denoising variable and define the exact Rao--Blackwell label by
\[
  \bar Z(X,Y,u)=\mathbb E[H\mid X,Y,u]
  =Y(1-Y)\,\partial_Y\log k_u(Y\mid X).
\]
For the permitted later-state information $W$, write
\[
  \mu(y)=y(1-y),\qquad
  m(W)=\mathbb E[\bar Z\mid W]=\mu(y)q(W).
\]
The boundary-tangent model predicts a finite coefficient
$q_\theta=q_B+q_{\theta,\mathrm{res}}$ and returns
$m_\theta=\mu q_\theta$. It minimizes the same direct, unweighted objective
\[
  \mathcal L(\theta)
  =\frac{\operatorname{mean}(\mu q_\theta-\bar Z)^2}{c^2},
  \qquad c^2=\operatorname{mean}_{\mathrm{train}}\bar Z^2.
\]
In particular, no quotient target $\bar Z/\mu$ is formed. The training-only
cellwise baseline is the direct least-squares coefficient
\[
  q_B=\frac{\sum \mu\bar Z}{\sum \mu^2}.
\]

\paragraph{Exact frozen-score logistic flow.}
During a learned substep, freeze $q$ and solve
\[
  \frac{dy}{du}=2q\,y(1-y).
\]
For $0<y<1$ the exact solution is
\[
  \operatorname{logit}(y^+)
  =\operatorname{logit}(y)+2q\,\delta u,
\]
while $y=0$ and $y=1$ remain fixed. Hence the update stays on the closed
pair simplex without clipping, flooring, limiting, projection, or
renormalization. Its local expansion is
\[
  y^+=y+2\mu(y)q\,\delta u+O(\delta u^2)
     =y+2m\,\delta u+O(\delta u^2),
\]
so it preserves the intended infinitesimal learned reverse generator.

\section{Quartile-specialized exact-RB learnability gate}

\paragraph{Motivation and unchanged target.}
The sealed v3 adjudication resolved the earliest forward-time quartile,
found a positive but underpowered direction in the second quartile, and found
positive alignment but excessive prediction energy in the final two
quartiles.  The next experiment therefore replaces the single shared learner
by four independent width-32 boundary-tangent experts.  Expert $q$ is trained
only on rows in forward-time quartile $q$, but every expert retains the exact
raw Jacobi/Rao--Blackwell label
\[
  \bar Z(X,Y,u)=Y(1-Y)\,\partial_Y\log k_u(Y\mid X)
\]
and minimizes plain, unweighted MSE within its quartile.  One positive
training-only RMS constant per quartile merely normalizes optimizer units; it
does not alter the population minimizer.

\paragraph{Training-only amplitude calibration.}
The first two experts have gain one.  For a frozen checkpoint in quartile
$q\in\{2,3\}$, a disjoint gain panel computes
\[
  C_q=\operatorname{mean}(\bar Z m_q),\qquad
  P_q=\operatorname{mean}(m_q^2),\qquad
  \lambda_q=C_q/P_q.
\]
The candidate is admissible only when $C_q,P_q>0$ and
$0<\lambda_q<1$; the gain is never clipped or fitted on selection evidence.
All gains are sealed before a second disjoint training-only role ranks one
checkpoint for each quartile.  Since the experts have disjoint temporal
support, this independent ranking is the exact separable system-selection
rule and does not search a Cartesian product on audit data.

\paragraph{Fresh evidence and authorization.}
One sealed four-expert system is evaluated first on 384 fresh whole paths and,
only after a pass, on 384 untouched confirmation paths.  Each audit uses the
same one-sided 99.5\% studentized max-$T$ family of six pooled contrasts: four
specialist-versus-zero quartile contrasts and two shrunken-versus-unit-gain
contrasts for quartiles 2 and 3.  Fifty thousand precommitted Philox bootstrap
replicates are shared across the six components.  Fixed phase, midpoint, and
phase-by-midpoint directional screens test compatibility without selecting or
modifying the system.
